SurveyFlow ("SurveyFlow", "we", "us", or "our") is a surveying, GIS mapping, GPS field-data and land-management application developed by Newton Mutuma.

This Privacy Policy explains how SurveyFlow collects, uses, stores, protects and shares information when you use the SurveyFlow mobile application and related services.

Developer: Newton Mutuma
Application: SurveyFlow
Country: Kenya
Privacy Contact: newtonmutuma007@gmail.com

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, SurveyFlow may collect or process the following categories of information.

1.1 Account Information

If you create or use a SurveyFlow account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Account authentication information
  • User role or permissions
  • Information necessary to maintain your account

Account information is used to authenticate users, manage access to SurveyFlow features, maintain project ownership and provide application services.

1.2 Location and GPS Information

SurveyFlow may request access to your device's location when you use GPS, mapping, surveying, stakeout or other location-based features.

This may include:

  • Latitude
  • Longitude
  • GPS accuracy
  • Altitude/elevation
  • Speed
  • Heading/direction
  • Location timestamps
  • Survey points and geographic coordinates

Location information is used to provide mapping, surveying, field-data collection, navigation, coordinate and stakeout functionality.

1.3 Survey and Project Data

When you create or upload information through SurveyFlow, the application may process:

  • Survey points
  • Control points
  • Coordinates
  • Land parcel information
  • Parcel boundaries
  • Areas and perimeters
  • Project information
  • Stakeout information
  • GIS data
  • Measurements
  • Survey observations
  • Maps and geographic features
  • Documents or files uploaded by you

1.4 Device and Technical Information

SurveyFlow or its supporting services may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the application, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Network information
  • Error or diagnostic information
  • Authentication and session information

1.5 Files and Documents

If you choose to upload files or documents to SurveyFlow, the application may process those files for the functionality you request.

We do not access files on your device unless you select or otherwise provide them to the application or grant a permission required for a particular feature.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through SurveyFlow to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide surveying and GIS functionality
  • Display maps and geographic information
  • Capture and process GPS information
  • Manage survey points and control points
  • Manage land parcels and project information
  • Provide stakeout and field-survey functionality
  • Synchronize information between supported devices and services
  • Store and retrieve project information
  • Process uploaded documents and files
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain application security
  • Detect and troubleshoot technical problems
  • Improve application reliability and functionality
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Location Data

SurveyFlow uses device location when necessary for location-based features that you choose to use.

Examples include:

  • GPS surveying
  • Mapping your current position
  • Recording survey points
  • Stakeout
  • Location-based field measurements
  • Geographic project workflows

You can control location permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling location permission may prevent certain SurveyFlow features from working.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal or sensitive user data.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide SurveyFlow services or comply with legal requirements.

4.1 Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers that provide infrastructure, hosting, database, authentication, storage, security or other technical services required to operate SurveyFlow.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal processes, protect rights or safety, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security and integrity of SurveyFlow.

4.3 User-Directed Sharing

If SurveyFlow provides functionality allowing you to export, download, transmit or otherwise share your own project or survey information, that sharing occurs at your direction.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through SurveyFlow against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Where applicable, information transmitted between the application and our services is protected using secure communications such as HTTPS/TLS.

Authentication credentials are not stored in plain text.

However, no internet-connected system or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SurveyFlow services, maintain accounts and projects, meet legitimate operational requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it or securely dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Account and Data Deletion

If you have a SurveyFlow account, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting:

newtonmutuma007@gmail.com

Your request should include the email address or username associated with your SurveyFlow account.

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize applicable account information and associated personal data, subject to information that we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

8. Your Permissions and Choices

SurveyFlow requests permissions only when they are needed for particular application functionality.

Depending on your device and the features you use, you may be asked to grant:

  • Location permission
  • Files or document access
  • Other permissions required by specific SurveyFlow features

You can manage permissions through your Android device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not function correctly.

9. Children's Privacy

SurveyFlow is intended for professional, educational, surveying, GIS, engineering, construction, land-management and related users.

SurveyFlow is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

SurveyFlow may use third-party services or software components necessary for functions such as hosting, authentication, databases, maps, location services, application infrastructure, security and diagnostics.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Maps and Geographic Services

SurveyFlow may use mapping or geographic services to display maps, geographic features, coordinates or location information.

When you use mapping functionality, certain technical or geographic information may be processed by the relevant mapping service according to its own terms and privacy policy.

SurveyFlow does not sell your geographic information.

12. Offline Data

Some SurveyFlow features may allow survey or project information to be stored locally on your device for offline use.

Locally stored information may remain on the device until you delete it, remove the relevant project, clear application data, uninstall the application or synchronize/delete the information through supported functionality.

13. User-Generated Survey Data

SurveyFlow may allow users to create professional survey and GIS information.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information they enter, upload, export or share through SurveyFlow is collected and used lawfully and that they have the necessary rights or permissions to process it.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SurveyFlow's features, data practices, legal requirements or services change.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy.
