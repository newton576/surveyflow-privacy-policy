SurveyFlow Account and Data Deletion

SurveyFlow users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your SurveyFlow account, send an email to the address below using the email address associated with your SurveyFlow account.

Email: newtonmutuma007@gmail.com

Please include your SurveyFlow account email or username and clearly state that you want your account deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

After the deletion request has been verified and processed, we will delete the SurveyFlow account and associated personal data, subject to information that we may be legally required to retain.

This may include:

  • Account information
  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Survey projects
  • Survey points and control points
  • Parcel and project data
  • Uploaded documents associated with the account
  • Other personal data associated with the account

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Processing Requests

We may need to verify that the deletion request was made by the account owner before processing it.

Once the request has been verified, the account and eligible associated data will be deleted in accordance with our applicable data retention practices.
